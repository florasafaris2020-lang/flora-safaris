01 — Superior Tents

Canvas walls, open sky.

The tents are the heart of the camp: simple, quiet and pitched so that each one has its own patch of silence.

Choose the setup that suits your party — a single traveller, a couple, three sharing, or a family. Rates differ only by occupancy, so the tent you book is the same whichever season you travel in.

  • Single, double, triple and family occupancy
  • Canvas safari tents, spread out for privacy
  • Low season from KSH 4,800 / USD 50 per night
Enquire about a tent
From KSH 5,000 · USD 55

02 — Deluxe Rooms

A room with a little more of everything.

Warm timber, softer light and more space to unpack — the deluxe rooms suit guests who like their comfort a shade more conventional.

They take the same four occupancies as the tents, which makes them an easy choice for families who would rather not split across rooms.

  • Single, double, triple and family occupancy
  • Solid-walled rooms with more floor space
  • Low season from KSH 5,000 / USD 55 per night
