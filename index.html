Where you stay Flora Safaris

Welcome

A small camp with a very large horizon.

The camp is a quiet place to come back to after a long day in the bush. Two ways to stay, a handful of rooms, and staff who know the rhythm of the place.

Flora Safaris looks after the booking side of things — the dates, the room, the rates and the questions you only think of at midnight. Tell us how you like to travel and we will shape the stay around it, from a single night on the road to a full week of early starts.
